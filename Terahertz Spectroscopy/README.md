# 🔬 Terahertz (THz) Spectroscopy of Materials

---

## 📌 Project Overview
This project focuses on the study and experimental/computational analysis of **Terahertz Spectroscopy** to investigate the complex optical and electromagnetic properties of materials. Terahertz radiation (0.1 THz to 10 THz) lies in the gap between microwaves and infrared light, making it a powerful non-destructive tool for probing low-energy material excitations, molecular vibrations, and carrier dynamics.

---

## 🔬 Key Objectives
- 📡 Analyze Time-Domain Terahertz Spectroscopy (THz-TDS) signatures.
- 📐 Extract material parameters such as complex refractive index $\tilde{n}(\omega)$, absorption coefficient $\alpha(\omega)$, and complex permittivity.
- 🔄 Process signal waveforms using Fourier Transform analysis to transition from time-domain signals to frequency-domain spectra.

---

## 🛠️ Tech Stack & Methods
- **Domain**: Condensed Matter Physics, Optics, Terahertz Photonics
- **Analysis Tools**: Python (NumPy, SciPy, Matplotlib) / MATLAB
- **Key Techniques**:
  - 📉 Signal Denoising & Fast Fourier Transform (FFT)
  - ⚡ Phase Extraction and Transmission Coefficient Calculation
